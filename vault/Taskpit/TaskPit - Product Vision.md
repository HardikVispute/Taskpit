# TaskPit - Product Vision

## One-sentence definition

TaskPit is an AI-native team execution platform where organizations coordinate members, assignments, deadlines, progress, submissions, reviews, and work history.

## Core principle

**TaskPit is the source of truth. AI is an interface and intelligence layer.**

Leaders should be able to work directly in the TaskPit web app or delegate through their preferred AI assistant connected using [[MCP Integration]]. The platform remains useful as a structured workspace even when an action does not involve AI.

## What the platform connects

- Organizations and their members.
- Tasks with owners, instructions, deadlines, and status.
- Assignment, progress, submission, review, revision, and completion.
- Work history and attribution.
- AI-assisted interactions through external assistants.
- Future project-aware context and member-facing assistance.

## Who it serves first

The initial audience is **student-organization leaders**, particularly people coordinating teams, events, and multiple responsibilities. Members are the other side of the workflow: they need to understand what is expected, know when it is due, and communicate progress or submit work.

## Product intent

TaskPit begins with [[Core Hypothesis - AI-Assisted Delegation]], while its broader direction addresses [[The Problem - Team Execution]]. The platform should make coordination clearer for both leaders and members, and create a more reliable record of work as the product evolves.

# Work History and Contribution Records

## Why it matters

Leaders have difficulty maintaining a reliable record of who actually did what. TaskPit should preserve useful work history so that contribution records are grounded in task events and outcomes, not just claims.

## Foundation

The first data model includes [[TaskEvent]], an append-only history of meaningful changes. This can preserve:
- Who created or reassigned a task.
- When key instructions or deadlines changed.
- Status changes.
- Later, who submitted work, reviewed it, requested changes, and approved completion.

## Attribution principles

- Distinguish assignment from contribution.
- Distinguish submission from acceptance.
- Preserve the role of reviewers and approvers.
- Avoid presenting an unreviewed claim as a verified contribution.
- Keep future recognition mechanisms grounded in transparent records.

The exact contribution formula, recognition experience, and certificate rules remain open product decisions.

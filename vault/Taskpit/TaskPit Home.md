## Current north star

Build a real, reliable first slice that tests whether a leader prefers delegating through their own AI assistant connected to TaskPit, with the resulting work becoming structured, editable, persistent records in TaskPit.

## Current stage

- **Stage:** Working prototype planning/build.
- **Initial audience:** Student-organization leaders.
- **Pilot:** Not started.
- **Prototype's primary question:** Does AI-assisted delegation through TaskPit feel more natural and convenient than the leader's current workflow of using AI to prepare task documents and distributing them manually?

# Prototype Validation

## Main question

Do student-organization leaders find delegating through their own AI assistant connected to TaskPit more natural and convenient than their existing workflow?

## Comparison baseline

The real current workflow is approximately: ask AI to prepare a task document, then distribute it manually. Validate the comparison against what each participant actually does.

## What to observe

- Can the leader understand how to connect and use the workflow?
- Can they describe work naturally without needing to learn a new task-entry ritual?
- Does the assistant ask useful questions when important information is missing?
- Are the created task records accurate?
- Can the leader find, inspect, and edit the resulting assignments?
- Is the connected workflow preferred over the existing process?
- What setup friction, confusion, or missing capabilities prevent use?

## Separate technical and product results

**Technical proof:** MCP creates authorized, persistent tasks; tasks appear correctly in the UI; editing and access controls work.

**Product evidence:** Leaders understand the workflow, find it valuable, and prefer it enough to use for real work.

Technical success alone is not evidence that people want the product. The pilot has not started yet, and the number of testers and formal success criteria are still to be defined.

## Suggested observation format

For each participant, record:
- Role and team context.
- Current delegation workflow.
- What they attempted.
- Where they hesitated or needed help.
- What they expected to happen.
- Whether they would use it for a real upcoming assignment and why.
- Their comparison with the existing process.
